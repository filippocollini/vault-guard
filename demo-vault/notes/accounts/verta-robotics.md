# Verta Robotics

Line-robotics OEM. Remote service connectivity across the installed base.
Deal: [[d-103-verta-service]]. Background: [[verta-board-review]].

**Numbers.** Service dispatches an engineer on site 340 times a year at €1,150
fully loaded. Remote diagnosis is expected to remove 60% of those: €234,600 a
year. Muraro built the case; Reuter approved it at board level.

**Who signs.** Anke Reuter, COO. Met three times, most recently 28 August on
site. Holds the 2026 capex budget and confirmed no further approval is needed
below €500,000.

**How they choose.** Criteria fixed in writing in the 12 May RFQ: session
start under 90 seconds, exportable per-machine log, support in Italian and
German, no VPN installed at the end customer.

**Process.** RFQ closed in May, shortlist of two in July, proof of concept
passed 20 August, board decision 15 October, signature by end of October.

**Paperwork.** Legal reviewed our MSA in July: two comments, both closed.
Procurement asks for 30 days after the decision. No separate security review.

**Pain.** Every fault that cannot be diagnosed remotely costs their end
customer around eight hours of stopped line, which Verta pays in SLA penalties.

**Champion.** Davide Muraro, Head of Service. He presented the business case to
the board on 3 September without us in the room.

**Competition.** Shortlisted against Rutger Systems, 20% cheaper, failed the
proof of concept on session start time (over four minutes).
