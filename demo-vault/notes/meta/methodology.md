# Qualification methodology

A deal is qualified along eight dimensions. An empty dimension **is the
finding**, not a gap in the paperwork — see [[northwind-marine]] for the
canonical example of a large deal with no confirmed buyer.

| Dimension | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| Metrics | Which number improves for the customer, and by how much? |
| Economic Buyer | Who signs? Have we met them? |
| Decision Criteria | What will they choose on? |
| Decision Process | Which steps and dates lead to signature? |
| Paper Process | Legal, procurement, security review — how long? |
| Identify Pain | What hurts, and what happens if they do nothing? |
| Champion | Who argues for us when we are not in the room? |
| Competition | Against whom, status quo included? |

Stages and their probabilities are defined in `schema.py`.
